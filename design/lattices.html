Lattice weights

kaldi lattices use LatticeWeight = (Value1, Value2) where Value1 is the graph cost (LM + transition probabilities) and Value2 is the acoustic cost -- both negated log-probabilities (lower = better). Keeping them separate is the whole point: rescoring or reweighting one side needs no re-decoding.

Semiring

Plus (alternative paths) minimizes Value1 + Value2; ties break by smaller Value1 (see Compare in fstext/lattice-weight.h). Times (concatenation) adds component-wise. So a lattice is a shortest-path problem over a pair of tropical costs.

Scaling

ScaleLattice(LatticeScale(lmwt, acwt), &lat) multiplies Value1 by lmwt and Value2 by acwt. kaldi conventions:

  • gmm-latgen-faster writes lattices with acoustic costs divided by --acoustic-scale (ScaleLattice(1, 1/acoustic_scale)) so the acoustic side is in raw log-likelihood units;

  • lattice-best-path --lm-scale=X --acoustic-scale=Y then finds the 1-best of the rescaled lattice: argmin(X*V1 + Y*V2).

kaldi_decoder.lattice_best_path() is exactly that core. The decode wrappers return lattices already scaled the kaldi way, so calling lattice_best_path(lat, lm_scale, 1.0) reproduces kaldi's scoring.

Why not determinize here

Determinization (DeterminizeLatticePhonePruned) merges equivalent alternative paths and is what kaldi writes to compact lattices. It does NOT change the 1-best, so this port leaves it out (the raw lattice is enough for best-path / n-best extraction); the option structs are still provided for callers that want to plug in determinization from kaldifst.

Alignment extraction

A lattice path's input labels are transition-ids (the alignment) and its output labels are words. GetLinearSymbolSequence linearizes a path FST into exactly those two sequences plus the total weight -- the single place all our outputs (words/alignments/likes) come from.
